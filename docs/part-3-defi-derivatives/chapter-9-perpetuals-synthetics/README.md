# Chapter 9: Perpetual Futures, Synthetics & Prediction Markets

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

    - Analyze the mechanics and regulatory classification of perpetual futures protocols
    - Distinguish synthetic assets from the instruments they track and identify the regulatory consequences
    - Evaluate prediction markets and event contracts under the CFTC's jurisdiction
    - Assess on-chain options protocols against traditional options regulation
    - Apply enforcement precedents from Polymarket and dYdX to current protocols

!!! warning "Key Terms"
    - **Perpetual Future (Perp)**: A derivative contract that tracks an underlying asset's price with no expiry date, using a funding rate mechanism to maintain price convergence
    - **Funding Rate**: A periodic payment between long and short position holders that incentivizes the perpetual's price to converge with the spot price
    - **Synthetic Asset**: A token designed to track the price of another asset without holding or delivering that asset
    - **Event Contract**: A binary derivative that pays based on whether a specified real-world event occurs
    - **Power Perpetual**: A derivative whose payoff tracks a power function of the underlying price (e.g., price-squared), such as Opyn's Squeeth

---

Chapter 8 established that DeFi protocols performing derivatives functions are subject to derivatives regulation. This chapter examines the four major categories of DeFi derivatives instruments — perpetual futures, synthetic assets, prediction markets, and options — analyzing the specific mechanics, regulatory classification, and enforcement history for each.

These are not abstract regulatory categories. Perpetual futures generate over $100 billion in daily trading volume across DeFi and centralized crypto exchanges. Prediction markets attracted hundreds of millions in volume during the 2024 U.S. election cycle. The protocols discussed in this chapter are the most used, most visible, and most enforcement-exposed products in DeFi.
