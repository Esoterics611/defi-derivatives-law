# Chapter 23: Review Questions

Test your understanding of autonomous compliance, AI-native regulation, DeFi-TradFi convergence, and career positioning in AI-augmented derivatives practice.

---

**Question 1: Compliance Trajectory**

Describe the three stages of AI compliance evolution (AI-assisted, AI-augmented, AI-autonomous). For each stage, identify one specific derivatives compliance function that exemplifies that stage and explain why it fits.

---

**Question 2: Compliance Oracle Design**

Design a compliance oracle system for a DeFi perpetual futures protocol. Specify: (a) what compliance checks the oracle performs, (b) what data sources it needs, (c) how it interacts with the protocol's smart contracts, and (d) what happens when a transaction fails a compliance check.

---

**Question 3: Machine-Readable Regulation**

The CFTC decides to publish position limit rules (17 CFR Part 150) in machine-readable format. What data fields would need to be encoded? How would a DeFi protocol consume this data to enforce position limits automatically? What are the risks of relying on machine-readable rules versus human-interpreted rules?

---

**Question 4: Regulatory AI Arms Race**

Explain the "asymmetry warning" from Section 23.2. If the CFTC deploys AI for market surveillance and a DeFi protocol relies on manual compliance, what specific types of violations is the CFTC's AI most likely to detect that manual compliance would miss?

---

**Question 5: DeFi-TradFi Convergence Scenario**

A major DeFi perpetual futures protocol with $2 billion in daily trading volume receives a letter from the CFTC stating that it appears to be operating as an unregistered swap execution facility. Outline three possible responses: (a) register as a SEF, (b) restructure to avoid SEF classification, (c) challenge the CFTC's jurisdiction. For each, identify the key regulatory arguments and the AI tools that would support the response.

---

**Question 6: Compliance Infrastructure Gap**

Select three rows from the "Compliance Infrastructure Gap" table in Section 23.3 and for each, describe a specific technical implementation that bridges the gap between DeFi architecture and TradFi regulatory requirements. Be specific about the technology, the data flows, and the human oversight requirements.

---

**Question 7: Career Positioning**

You have completed this book and are choosing between two job offers: (a) Head of Compliance at a mid-size DeFi derivatives protocol with $500M TVL, and (b) Digital Asset Compliance Analyst at a top-5 investment bank. Compare these roles across: regulatory exposure, career growth, compensation structure, skill development, and professional risk. Which would you choose and why?

---

**Question 8: Portfolio Building**

Design a compliance gap assessment project (one of the portfolio-building projects from Section 23.4) for a specific DeFi protocol of your choice. Specify: (a) which protocol and why, (b) which CFTC regulations you would assess against, (c) how you would use AI tools in the assessment, (d) how you would structure the deliverable, and (e) what ethical considerations apply (since you're publishing analysis about a real protocol).

---

**Question 9: Convergence Prediction**

Based on the institutional adoption milestones in Section 23.3, predict which milestone will occur next and why. What regulatory changes would need to happen for it to proceed? What role would AI play in enabling it?

---

**Question 10: Synthesis**

Looking back across all 23 chapters, identify the single most important insight from each of the six Parts that you would include in a one-page brief for a DeFi protocol founder who is about to launch a derivatives product. Use AI to draft the brief, then explain what you would verify and revise before delivering it.
