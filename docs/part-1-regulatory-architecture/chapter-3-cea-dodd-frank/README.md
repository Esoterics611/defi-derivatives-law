# Chapter 3: The Commodity Exchange Act & Dodd-Frank Title VII

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this chapter, you will be able to:
    - Navigate the structure of the Commodity Exchange Act and identify key provisions
    - Explain how Dodd-Frank Title VII revolutionized swap regulation after the 2008 crisis
    - Distinguish between exempt and excluded instruments under the CEA
    - Apply the swap vs. security-based swap vs. mixed swap taxonomy to classify any instrument
    - Understand why the 2008 financial crisis — specifically AIG — led directly to the regulatory framework that now governs DeFi derivatives


!!! warning "Key Terms"
    - **Commodity Exchange Act (CEA)**: 7 U.S.C. §§1–27f, the foundational statute governing U.S. derivatives markets
    - **Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform Act**: The 2010 statute that overhauled financial regulation post-2008; Title VII covers OTC derivatives
    - **Title VII**: The section of Dodd-Frank that established the comprehensive swap regulatory framework
    - **Eligible Contract Participant (ECP)**: A sophisticated entity or individual meeting financial thresholds, eligible for less restrictive treatment under the CEA
    - **Mixed Swap**: A derivative with characteristics of both a swap (CFTC) and a security-based swap (SEC)
    - **Excluded Commodity**: Financial commodities (interest rates, currencies, indices) excluded from certain CEA provisions
    - **Exempt Commodity**: All commodities other than excluded commodities and agricultural commodities


---

The Commodity Exchange Act is the statute. Dodd-Frank Title VII is the revolution. Together, they create the legal framework that governs every derivative instrument in the United States — and, increasingly, every DeFi protocol that creates derivative-like instruments accessible to U.S. persons.

This chapter gives you the statutory map. When you encounter a regulatory question in Chapters 4–23, you'll know exactly where in the CEA or Dodd-Frank to find the answer — or, more practically, where to direct your AI to find it.
