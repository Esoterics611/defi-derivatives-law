# Chapter 18: Review Questions

---

**1.** Explain the three levels of CFTC position limits (spot month, single month, all months combined). Why are spot month limits the tightest? What market manipulation risk do they specifically address?

**2.** The aggregation requirement under CFTC Rule 150.4 requires combining positions across accounts under common ownership or control. A hedge fund manager operates three funds with separate trading strategies and separate risk budgets. Must their positions be aggregated? Under what conditions might they qualify for an exemption?

**3.** A corn farmer holds 5,000 bushels of physical corn inventory and wants to hedge by selling corn futures. Explain how the bona fide hedging exemption under Rule 150.1 would apply. What documentation would the farmer need to provide? What happens if the farmer sells more futures than needed to hedge the physical inventory?

**4.** Describe the CFTC's large trader reporting framework under Part 18. What is the relationship between reportable position levels and position limits? Why does the CFTC set reportable levels well below position limits?

**5.** Explain the purpose and content of Form 40. If a DeFi trader accumulates a large position on a CFTC-regulated derivatives exchange, would they be required to file Form 40? What if their position is entirely on unregulated DeFi protocols?

**6.** Interpret the following hypothetical COT data for WTI crude oil futures: Commercial net position is -180,000 contracts (extreme short). Non-commercial (speculative) net position is +165,000 contracts (extreme long). What does this positioning suggest about the market? What historical pattern does extreme positioning typically precede?

**7.** Explain the DeFi position transparency paradox. Why does the system with the most transparent position data (DeFi) have the least regulatory reporting? What would need to change for DeFi position data to be useful to regulators?

**8.** A single DeFi trader controls 200 wallet addresses and holds $50 million in aggregate perpetual futures positions across three protocols. No single address holds more than $500,000. Explain why per-address position limits fail here. Describe two approaches that could detect this concentration.

**9.** Design a "Regulatory Transparency Layer" for a DeFi perpetual futures protocol. Specify: (a) what data is collected, (b) how wallet clustering identifies entities, (c) what thresholds trigger alerts, (d) what public reports are published, and (e) what level of the Position Transparency Spectrum your design achieves.

**10.** The CFTC is considering extending position limit requirements to DeFi derivatives protocols. Draft a comment letter arguing either for or against this extension. Address: technical feasibility, the multi-wallet problem, competitive impact, and whether existing on-chain transparency is a sufficient substitute for formal reporting.
