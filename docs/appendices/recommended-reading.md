# Appendix F: Recommended Reading

Curated resources for continued learning across derivatives law, CFTC practice, DeFi regulation, AI in legal practice, and ISDA documentation. Resources are organized by topic and rated by difficulty level.

---

## Derivatives Law Fundamentals

| Title | Author | Why It Matters | Level |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| *Regulation of the Derivatives Markets* | Philip McBride Johnson & Thomas Lee Hazen | The treatise on US derivatives regulation; comprehensive coverage of CEA, CFTC rules, and case law | Advanced |
| *The Law of Securities Regulation* (derivatives chapters) | Thomas Lee Hazen | Covers the SEC/CFTC jurisdictional divide and security-based swaps | Intermediate |
| *Derivatives Law and Regulation* | Gary E. Kalbaugh | Accessible law school casebook covering core derivatives regulation concepts with problem sets | Intermediate |
| *Financial Derivatives: Pricing and Risk Management* | Robert Kolb & James Overdahl | Economic foundations of derivatives — essential for understanding what the law regulates | Intro |
| *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* | John C. Hull | The definitive textbook on derivatives pricing and mechanics; essential background for regulatory analysis | Intro-Intermediate |

## CFTC-Specific Practice

| Title | Author | Why It Matters | Level |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| *CFTC Annual Reports* | CFTC | Annual overview of enforcement priorities, rulemaking activity, and market developments; free at CFTC.gov | Intro |
| *CFTC Staff Letters and Guidance* | CFTC Staff | No-action letters and interpretive guidance that provide practical insight into how the CFTC applies its rules | Intermediate |
| *Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform: Title VII — OTC Derivatives* | Congressional Research Service | Clear, non-partisan explanation of Dodd-Frank's derivatives reforms | Intro |
| *"Swap" Regulation Under the Commodity Exchange Act* | Practical Law (Westlaw) | Practitioner guide to swap classification and regulation with decision trees and checklists | Intermediate |
| *Position Limits for Derivatives* | CFTC Final Rule, 86 Fed. Reg. 3236 (Jan. 14, 2021) | The current position limits framework; essential reading for understanding Part 150 | Advanced |

## DeFi Regulation

| Title | Author | Why It Matters | Level |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| *DeFi and the Future of Finance* | Campbell Harvey, Ashwin Ramachandran, Joey Santoro | Foundational overview of DeFi from a finance perspective; good for understanding what regulators are looking at | Intro |
| *"Digital Assets, Distributed Ledger Technology, and the Future of Capital Markets"* | CFTC Technology Advisory Committee Reports | CFTC's own analysis of digital asset regulation; primary source for understanding the regulator's perspective | Intermediate |
| *"Statement on DeFi Risks, Regulations, and Opportunities"* | IOSCO (International Organization of Securities Commissions) | Global regulatory perspective on DeFi risks; influential on US regulatory thinking | Intermediate |
| CFTC v. Ooki DAO — Court Opinion | Judge William Orrick, N.D. Cal. | The single most important court decision on DAO regulatory liability; must-read for DeFi governance | Advanced |
| *"Crypto Assets and Decentralized Finance Through a Financial Stability Lens"* | Financial Stability Board (FSB) | Global standard-setter's analysis of DeFi risks; shapes regulatory approaches worldwide | Intermediate |
| *Mastering Ethereum* | Andreas Antonopoulos & Gavin Wood | Technical foundation for understanding smart contracts and DeFi protocol architecture | Intro-Intermediate |

## AI and Legal Practice

| Title | Author | Why It Matters | Level |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| *"Generative AI and the Legal Profession"* | American Bar Association | ABA guidance on ethical use of AI in legal practice; directly relevant to AI-augmented compliance work | Intro |
| *"Artificial Intelligence and the Practice of Law"* | Various state bar associations (NY, CA, TX) | State-level ethics opinions on AI use in legal work; evolving guidance on disclosure and supervision obligations | Intermediate |
| *Legal Prompt Engineering: Principles and Practices* | Various (emerging field — search for current resources) | Practical guides to prompting AI for legal research and analysis; the field is evolving rapidly | Intro-Intermediate |
| *Re: Mata v. Avianca* (S.D.N.Y. 2023) | Judge P. Kevin Castel | The cautionary tale: attorneys sanctioned for submitting AI-generated briefs with fabricated case citations; essential reading on verification obligations | Intro |
| *Anthropic's Claude Documentation* | Anthropic | Technical documentation for the AI model most suited to legal analysis; understanding model capabilities improves prompt engineering | Intro |

## ISDA and Documentation

| Title | Author | Why It Matters | Level |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| *Mastering the ISDA Master Agreements (1992 and 2002)* | Paul Harding | The standard practitioner guide to ISDA documentation; section-by-section analysis of the Master Agreement | Intermediate |
| *ISDA Master Agreement — Practical Guide* | Practical Law (Westlaw) | Shorter practitioner guide with negotiation checklists and market standard analysis | Intermediate |
| *User's Guide to the 2002 ISDA Master Agreement* | ISDA | ISDA's own guide to its documentation; official interpretation of provisions | Intermediate |
| *ISDA Common Domain Model (CDM) Documentation* | ISDA / Rosetta Technology | Technical specification for machine-readable derivatives documentation; essential for AI integration | Advanced |
