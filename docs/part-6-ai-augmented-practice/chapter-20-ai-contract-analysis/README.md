# Chapter 20: AI for Contract Analysis & Drafting

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

    - Parse ISDA documentation using AI with structured extraction workflows
    - Use AI to compare, negotiate, and redline ISDA Schedules and CSAs
    - Audit DeFi smart contracts for derivatives-regulatory compliance using AI
    - Draft compliance documents, risk disclosures, and regulatory filings with AI assistance
    - Apply battle-tested prompt templates for each contract analysis task

!!! warning "Key Terms"
    - **Document Parsing**: Structured extraction of elections, modifications, thresholds, and defined terms from ISDA documentation
    - **Schedule Negotiation**: The process of agreeing on modifications to the standard ISDA Master Agreement — the Schedule is where the real business terms live
    - **Smart Contract Audit**: Reviewing Solidity (or other smart contract) code for security vulnerabilities, economic logic errors, and regulatory compliance issues
    - **First Draft Workflow**: Using AI to generate an 80% complete document that a human expert refines to 100% — dramatically faster than drafting from scratch

---

ISDA documentation is the most formulaic and complex genre of legal writing. A typical bilateral derivatives relationship involves a Master Agreement (30+ pages of standard terms), a Schedule (20-40 pages of elections and modifications), a Credit Support Annex (15-25 pages of collateral terms), multiple Confirmations (each 5-15 pages), and references to ISDA Definitions booklets running hundreds of pages. Reviewing this documentation stack for a single counterparty relationship takes an experienced derivatives lawyer 8-15 hours.

This makes ISDA documentation the **ideal domain** for AI-augmented practice. The documents are highly structured, use standardized language, follow predictable patterns, and require cross-reference analysis across multiple related documents. AI models excel at exactly these tasks — and they do them in minutes rather than hours.

This chapter provides the practical workflows. Section 20.1 covers parsing ISDA documents for structured data extraction. Section 20.2 addresses Schedule negotiation with AI-assisted comparison and redlining. Section 20.3 bridges to DeFi with AI-assisted smart contract auditing for derivatives compliance. Section 20.4 covers drafting compliance documents with AI. The chapter concludes with a library of battle-tested prompt templates.

The approach throughout is practical: each section provides a step-by-step workflow, specific prompts, expected outputs, and — critically — what the human must verify. AI is a 10x accelerator, not a replacement for expertise. The judgment call is still yours.
