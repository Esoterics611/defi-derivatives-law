# 20.1 Parsing ISDA Documentation with AI

!!! info "Section Objectives"
    After completing this section, you will be able to:

    - Identify the ISDA documentation stack and what each document contains
    - Execute a structured AI-assisted ISDA Schedule review workflow
    - Extract elections, modifications, and threshold amounts from complex documents
    - Verify AI extraction against source documents

---

## The ISDA Documentation Stack

Every bilateral OTC derivatives relationship is governed by a documentation stack — multiple interlocking documents that together define the legal and economic terms:

| Document | Pages | What It Contains | AI Extraction Value |
|---------|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Master Agreement** (2002 or 1992) | ~30 | Standard boilerplate: definitions, obligations, events of default, early termination, close-out | Low — standard text that rarely changes. AI useful for confirming which version is in use |
| **Schedule** | 20-40 | Elections, modifications, added provisions — THIS is where the custom terms live | **Very High** — the Schedule is dense, cross-referential, and contains every business-critical election |
| **Credit Support Annex (CSA)** | 15-25 | Collateral terms: eligible collateral, thresholds, minimum transfer amounts, valuation timing | **High** — structured elections with numeric values, collateral schedules, and haircuts |
| **Confirmations** | 5-15 each | Transaction-specific terms: notional, dates, rates, payment frequencies, calculation methodology | **High** — standardized fields that map directly to trade booking systems |
| **Definitions booklets** | 50-200+ | Standardized definitions for each product type (2006 Definitions, 2021 Interest Rate Definitions, etc.) | Medium — reference documents; AI useful for looking up specific defined terms |

## The AI Parsing Workflow

### Step 1: Document Ingestion

Feed the document to the AI model with explicit structural instructions. ISDA Schedules are organized by Parts (Part 1: Termination Provisions, Part 2: Tax Representations, Part 3: Agreement to Deliver Documents, Part 4: Miscellaneous, Part 5: Other Provisions). Tell the AI what structure to expect.

**System prompt:**
```
You are a derivatives documentation specialist reviewing an ISDA Master Agreement Schedule. The Schedule modifies the standard 2002 ISDA Master Agreement through elections and amendments organized in Parts 1-5. Your task is to extract every election, modification, threshold amount, and added provision into a structured format.
```

### Step 2: Structured Extraction

Ask the AI to extract elections into a standardized table format:

**User prompt:**
```
Parse this ISDA Schedule and extract all elections into a structured table with columns:
1. Part/Section reference
2. Master Agreement provision being modified
3. Election or modification made
4. Counterparty-specific details (if different for Party A vs Party B)
5. Threshold amounts or numeric values
6. Cross-references to other documents or provisions

Flag any provisions that deviate from market standard for [counterparty type: dealer-to-dealer / dealer-to-fund / dealer-to-corporate].
```

### Step 3: Critical Election Extraction

Key elections that must be captured accurately:

| Election | Master Agreement Section | Why It Matters | Common AI Error |
|----------|------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Specified Entity | §14 | Defines which affiliates are covered — affects cross-default and credit events | AI often lists the entity names but misses the scope limitations |
| Cross-Default Threshold | §5(a)(vi) | Amount that triggers cross-default — critical credit risk parameter | AI may extract the dollar amount but miss whether it applies to both parties or just one |
| Automatic Early Termination | §6(a) | Whether AET is elected and for which parties — especially important for non-US entities | AI frequently misreads conditional AET elections (e.g., "elected only if Party A is not subject to US insolvency proceedings") |
| Governing Law | §13(a) | English law vs. New York law — determines which courts hear disputes | Straightforward but AI sometimes conflates governing law with jurisdiction |
| Credit Support Provider | Part 1(f) | Guarantors — whose credit supports the counterparty's obligations | AI may miss that the Credit Support Provider is a different entity than the party |
| Threshold Amount for AET | §6(a) | Threshold below which AET automatically terminates all transactions | AI sometimes confuses this with the Cross-Default Threshold |

### Step 4: Human Verification

This is not optional. AI extraction must be verified against the source document for:

**Cross-references.** ISDA Schedules constantly cross-reference: "Section 5(a)(vi) is amended by replacing '$100,000,000' with 'the amount set forth in Part 5(j) below.'" AI models sometimes capture the reference but not the target. Trace every cross-reference to its resolution.

**Conditional provisions.** Many elections are conditional: "Automatic Early Termination shall apply to Party B only if Party B becomes subject to proceedings under [list of non-US insolvency regimes]." AI models may capture "AET applies to Party B" without the condition.

**Defined terms.** Schedules define bespoke terms in Part 5 that are used elsewhere in the document. If the AI encounters "the Applicable Threshold" without resolving its definition, the extraction is incomplete.

**Amendments to the Master Agreement text.** Part 5 often contains direct amendments: "Section 6(e) is deleted in its entirety and replaced with [...]." These are the most important provisions and the most frequently mishandled by AI — the model must capture the full replacement text, not just the fact that an amendment was made.

!!! info "AI Practitioner Note: System Prompt for ISDA Analysis"
    For best results, provide the AI with context about the counterparty relationship:

    *"You are reviewing an ISDA Schedule between [Party A type: major dealer] and [Party B type: mid-market hedge fund]. The governing Master Agreement is the 2002 ISDA. Standard market practice for this counterparty pair is: [brief description of typical elections]. Extract all elections and flag any that deviate from market standard. For each deviation, explain the business significance and which party it favors."*

    The counterparty context dramatically improves the AI's ability to identify non-standard provisions — the model needs to know what "standard" looks like to flag deviations.


!!! warning ""
    **AI Limitation Warning:** AI models frequently misinterpret cross-references between ISDA Schedule modifications and Master Agreement provisions. A Schedule may say: "The definition of 'Specified Transaction' in Section 14 is amended to include [additional language]." The AI may extract the amendment but not correctly trace which defined term is affected or how the amended definition interacts with other provisions that reference it. Always manually trace cross-references through the document chain.


### Before/After: ISDA Schedule Review

| Metric | Without AI | With AI |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Time to review 40-page Schedule | 3-4 hours | 30-45 minutes |
| Election extraction completeness | High (experienced lawyer) | 85-90% (requires verification) |
| Cross-reference tracing | Manual, error-prone for juniors | Automated but needs human confirmation |
| Non-standard provision identification | Depends on lawyer's experience | Consistent — flags against specified market standard |
| Deliverable: structured summary | 1-2 hours to draft | Generated in seconds, refined in 15 minutes |

## Section Summary

- The ISDA documentation stack (Master Agreement, Schedule, CSA, Confirmations, Definitions) contains thousands of interrelated provisions
- AI excels at structured extraction from ISDA Schedules: elections, thresholds, modifications, and cross-references
- The workflow is: ingest → structured extraction → critical election focus → human verification
- AI reduces Schedule review from 3-4 hours to 30-45 minutes, but human verification of cross-references and conditional provisions is mandatory
- Provide counterparty context in the system prompt to enable non-standard provision identification
