# Part VI: AI-Augmented Derivatives Practice

Parts I through V gave you the legal knowledge. You understand the CEA, Dodd-Frank, ISDA architecture, DeFi derivatives classification, compliance program design, and the CFTC's enforcement playbook. You know more about derivatives law than most DeFi protocol operators — and more about DeFi than most derivatives lawyers.

Part VI teaches you to **deploy** that knowledge through AI.

The thesis is simple: the future derivatives practitioner doesn't work harder. They work with AI agents that handle the volume — parsing 40-page ISDA Schedules, monitoring regulatory changes across eight jurisdictions, analyzing enforcement patterns, auditing smart contract code, drafting compliance documents. The human provides what AI cannot: judgment, context, strategic thinking, and quality control.

This is not speculative. AI-augmented legal practice is already happening at major law firms, compliance departments, and financial institutions. The tools exist. The workflows are proven. What's missing is the bridge between general AI capability and derivatives-specific application. Part VI builds that bridge.

Chapter 20 covers **AI for contract analysis and drafting** — the most immediately practical application. ISDA documentation is the ideal AI domain: massive, standardized, cross-reference heavy, and formulaic. We provide step-by-step workflows and battle-tested prompt templates for parsing Schedules, comparing CSAs, negotiating terms, auditing smart contracts, and drafting compliance documents.

Chapter 21 covers **AI for regulatory monitoring and research** — the strategic application. Derivatives regulation changes constantly: new CFTC rules, enforcement actions, no-action letters, cross-border developments. AI transforms passive monitoring into active intelligence, automatically detecting changes, assessing their impact, and drafting response plans.

Chapter 22 covers **AI agents for derivatives workflow automation** — the frontier. Autonomous AI agents that execute multi-step legal research, compliance monitoring, and document preparation with minimal human intervention.

Chapter 23 looks ahead to the **future of AI-augmented derivatives practice** — where the technology, regulation, and market are heading.

The goal of Part VI: make you a 10x derivatives practitioner. Not by working 10x harder, but by managing AI systems that do the volume work while you provide the judgment that makes it correct.
