# Chapter 5: Review Questions

!!! info "Instructions"
    These questions test both conceptual understanding and applied analysis. Questions marked with 🤖 are designed to be completed using AI assistance.


---

## Conceptual Questions

**1.** Explain the four-prong swap dealer test. For each prong, provide one example of a traditional finance entity that would satisfy it and one hypothetical DeFi entity or protocol that might satisfy it.

**2.** What is the distinction between "dealing" (entering swaps to accommodate counterparty demand) and "trading" (entering swaps for one's own account)? Why is this distinction critical for the de minimis threshold calculation?

**3.** The Eligible Contract Participant (ECP) requirement makes it illegal for non-ECPs to enter swaps off-exchange. Explain why this requirement is particularly problematic for permissionless DeFi protocols. What is the minimum net worth for an individual to qualify as an ECP?

**4.** Compare the CFTC's margin framework for uncleared swaps (IM + VM) with a typical DeFi perpetual futures protocol's collateral and liquidation system. In what ways does DeFi exceed the TradFi framework, and in what ways does it fall short?

**5.** Why have zero entities registered as Major Swap Participants despite the designation existing since 2012? What does this tell you about the effectiveness of threshold-based regulation?

## Applied Analysis

**6.** A crypto-native trading firm provides continuous two-sided quotes in BTC and ETH derivatives through bilateral OTC agreements with institutional counterparties. Over the past 12 months, the firm has entered derivatives with $6.5 billion in aggregate gross notional. Analyze: (a) which prong(s) of the swap dealer test this firm likely satisfies, (b) whether it exceeds the de minimis threshold, (c) what happens if its volume increases 30% next year.

**7.** A DeFi perpetual futures protocol operates as follows: users deposit collateral, take leveraged long/short positions, and the protocol's liquidity pool serves as counterparty. The protocol processes $500 million in notional volume daily. Analyze which CFTC business conduct requirements from Section 5.3 the protocol could theoretically satisfy, which it cannot, and what structural changes would be needed for compliance.

**8.** A DAO treasury holds $200 million in assets and has entered $1.5 billion in notional swap-equivalent positions across various DeFi protocols to hedge its token exposure and generate yield. Analyze whether the DAO could trigger MSP designation under Category 1 (substantial position). Which swap category(ies) would apply? Does the hedging exemption help?

## 🤖 AI-Assisted Questions

**9.** 🤖 Prompt an AI to draft a **compliance checklist** for a newly registered non-bank swap dealer. The checklist should cover: (a) NFA membership, (b) capital requirements, (c) margin rules, (d) business conduct standards (external and internal), (e) recordkeeping, (f) reporting, and (g) CCO designation. Verify each item against the CFTC rules cited in this chapter. Identify any requirements the AI missed and any it fabricated.

**10.** 🤖 Provide an AI with the Archegos scenario: *"A single entity has $10 billion in equity and has accumulated total return swap positions with 6 different prime brokers, with aggregate notional of $36 billion concentrated in 5 stocks. No prime broker knows about the positions with the others."* Ask the AI to: (a) identify all regulatory violations or gaps, (b) propose specific regulatory reforms, and (c) analyze whether DeFi's on-chain transparency would have prevented this outcome. Evaluate whether the AI's proposed reforms match the actual SEC/CFTC proposals that followed the real Archegos collapse.

!!! warning "AI Verification Focus"
    For question 9, the most common AI errors are: (1) citing outdated capital requirements, (2) confusing bank SD and non-bank SD rules, (3) omitting the voice recording requirement, and (4) stating incorrect de minimis thresholds. Use the specific rules and thresholds from this chapter to verify.
