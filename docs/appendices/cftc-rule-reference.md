## Citation Format Guide

When citing CFTC regulatory authorities, use the following formats:

### Statutes
- **Commodity Exchange Act**: CEA § [section] or 7 U.S.C. § [section]
- Example: CEA § 1a(47) or 7 U.S.C. § 1a(47) — definition of "swap"

### Code of Federal Regulations
- **CFTC Rules**: 17 CFR § [Part].[Section]
- Example: 17 CFR § 23.431 — swap dealer business conduct rules

### Federal Register
- **Proposed/Final Rules**: [Volume] Fed. Reg. [Page] ([Date])
- Example: 77 Fed. Reg. 30596 (May 23, 2012) — Further Definition of "Swap Dealer"

### Enforcement Actions
- **Administrative**: In re [Respondent], CFTC Docket No. [Number] ([Year])
- Example: In re bZeroX, LLC, CFTC Docket No. 22-31 (2022)

### No-Action Letters
- **Staff Letters**: CFTC Letter No. [Number] ([Date])
- Example: CFTC Letter No. 19-17 (July 10, 2019)

### Interpretive Guidance
- **Staff Advisory**: CFTC Staff Advisory No. [Number] ([Date])
- **Interpretive Letter**: CFTC Interpretive Letter No. [Number] ([Date])
