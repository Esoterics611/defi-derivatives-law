# Part III: DeFi as Derivatives Market

!!! info "What This Part Covers"
    Parts I and II built the legal and regulatory framework — the statutes, the agencies, the definitions, the infrastructure requirements. This part applies all of it to the protocols you actually use.

---

This is the heart of the book. Everything in Parts I and II was preparation for this question: **when you interact with a DeFi protocol, are you participating in a regulated derivatives market?**

The answer, for most DeFi derivatives protocols, is yes. A perpetual futures platform is an unregistered designated contract market. A synthetic asset protocol is issuing unregistered swaps. A prediction market is offering unregistered event contracts. An options vault is writing unregistered commodity options. The CFTC has said this explicitly in enforcement actions, and courts have agreed.

Part III analyzes specific DeFi protocols through the regulatory lens built in Parts I and II. Chapter 8 establishes the "functional equivalence" framework — the principle that if a protocol performs the function of regulated derivatives infrastructure, it is subject to the same regulation regardless of its technical architecture. Chapter 9 examines specific instrument categories: perpetual futures, synthetic assets, prediction markets, and options protocols. Chapter 10 addresses liquidity provision and compliance obligations. Chapter 11 tackles DAO governance and the liability of token holders.

If you build, invest in, govern, or advise DeFi derivatives protocols, this is the part that tells you where you stand.
