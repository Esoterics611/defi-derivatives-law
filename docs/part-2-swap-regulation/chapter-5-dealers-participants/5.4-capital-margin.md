# 5.4 Capital, Margin & Segregation Requirements

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this section, you will be able to:
    - Distinguish between capital requirements for bank and non-bank swap dealers
    - Explain initial margin (IM), variation margin (VM), and the Uncleared Margin Rules (UMR)
    - Compare TradFi margin frameworks to DeFi over-collateralization and liquidation mechanics
    - Use AI to model margin requirements and identify where DeFi collateral systems diverge from regulatory expectations


---

## Capital Requirements for Swap Dealers

Swap dealers must maintain minimum capital to absorb potential losses. The CFTC's capital rules (finalized 2020, compliance required by 2024) establish different frameworks depending on the type of entity:

| SD Type | Capital Framework | Minimum Capital |
|---------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Bank SD** | Bank capital rules (OCC, Federal Reserve, FDIC) | Tier 1 capital ratios per Basel III |
| **Non-bank SD (net liquid assets approach)** | CFTC §23.101(a)(1)(i) | Greater of $20 million or 8% of uncleared swap margin |
| **Non-bank SD (tangible net worth approach)** | §23.101(a)(1)(ii) | Greater of $20 million or 8% of uncleared swap margin |
| **Non-bank SD (bank-based approach)** | §23.101(a)(2) | Basel III-like risk-weighted capital ratios |

!!! warning "For DeFi Entities"
    If a DeFi protocol operator or DAO were required to register as a swap dealer, it would need to demonstrate capital adequacy under one of these approaches. For most DeFi entities, this would require maintaining $20+ million in identifiable, encumbered capital — a requirement that is structurally difficult for entities whose "capital" consists of volatile governance tokens or pooled liquidity.


## Margin Requirements: The Post-2008 Safeguard

Margin is the collateral posted by swap counterparties to cover potential losses. Dodd-Frank mandated margin for both cleared and uncleared swaps — the most significant change in counterparty risk management since the 2008 crisis.

### Cleared Swaps

For swaps cleared through a DCO, the clearinghouse sets margin requirements:

- **Initial margin (IM):** Posted at trade inception; covers potential loss during the close-out period (typically 5-day liquidation horizon for cleared swaps)
- **Variation margin (VM):** Daily mark-to-market settlement; the losing party posts cash to the winning party every business day

### Uncleared Swaps: The Margin Rules

For swaps that are not cleared (still roughly 40% of the market by notional), CFTC Regulation §23.150–23.161 imposes margin requirements on swap dealers:

| Margin Type | When Collected | How Calculated | Acceptable Collateral |
|------------|---------------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Initial Margin (IM)** | At trade inception, adjusted periodically | Standardized schedule or approved internal model (10-day, 99% confidence VaR) | Cash, government securities, certain corporate bonds, equities (with haircuts) |
| **Variation Margin (VM)** | Daily | Mark-to-market of outstanding positions | Cash only (for Phase 1-5 entities) |

### Uncleared Margin Rules (UMR) Phase-In

The UMR were phased in from 2016 through 2022, progressively lowering the threshold of aggregate average notional amount (AANA) that triggers IM requirements:

| Phase | Compliance Date | AANA Threshold |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Phase 1 | Sept 2016 | $3 trillion |
| Phase 2 | Sept 2017 | $2.25 trillion |
| Phase 3 | Sept 2018 | $1.5 trillion |
| Phase 4 | Sept 2019 | $750 billion |
| Phase 5 | Sept 2021 | $50 billion |
| Phase 6 | Sept 2022 | $8 billion |

Phase 6 brought hundreds of additional entities into scope — including smaller funds, insurance companies, and corporate end-users.

### The IM Model

Under the standardized schedule (CFTC §23.154), IM is calculated as a percentage of notional:

| Asset Class | IM as % of Notional |
|------------|-------------------|
| Credit (0-2 year) | 2% |
| Credit (2-5 year) | 5% |
| Credit (5+ year) | 10% |
| Commodity | 15% |
| Equity | 15% |
| FX | 6% |
| Interest rate (0-2 year) | 1% |
| Interest rate (2-5 year) | 2% |
| Interest rate (5+ year) | 4% |
| Other | 15% |

Many large dealers use the **ISDA SIMM** (Standard Initial Margin Model) as an approved internal model, which typically produces lower IM requirements than the standardized schedule by recognizing portfolio-level diversification.

## DeFi Collateral: Parallel Architecture, Different Rules

DeFi has independently developed collateral and margin systems that serve the same economic function as TradFi margin — but with different mechanics:

| Feature | TradFi Margin | DeFi Collateral |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| Who sets requirements | Regulator (CFTC) or clearinghouse | Protocol (governance or algorithm) |
| Collateral types | Cash, government bonds, approved securities | Native tokens, stablecoins, yield-bearing assets |
| Margin calls | Daily bilateral exchange | Continuous, automated via oracle price feeds |
| Close-out process | 5-10 day negotiated liquidation period | Instant liquidation via liquidation bots |
| Segregation | Required — customer margin in segregated accounts | Pool-based — collateral typically commingled |
| Haircuts | Standardized (CFTC schedule or internal model) | Protocol-specific liquidation thresholds |

!!! success "Key Insight"
    DeFi's collateral systems are in some ways **more conservative** than TradFi margin: over-collateralization ratios of 150-200% are common (vs. 2-15% IM in TradFi), and liquidation is instant rather than delayed. But they lack the regulatory structure of segregation requirements, approved collateral schedules, and portfolio-level margin models that TradFi has developed over decades.


!!! info "AI Practitioner Note"
    Margin calculation is an excellent AI use case:

    *"Calculate the standardized initial margin requirement for the following uncleared swap portfolio: (1) 5-year interest rate swap, $100M notional, (2) 3-year commodity swap, $50M notional, (3) 2-year equity index swap, $75M notional. Use the CFTC standardized schedule (§23.154). Then calculate the net IM after applying the 60% diversification offset for non-correlated asset classes."*

    **What to verify:** AI handles the per-category calculation well but often misapplies the diversification offset. The CFTC allows a net-to-gross ratio adjustment for portfolios spanning multiple asset classes, but the specific methodology varies by model approval. Always check whether the AI is applying the correct offset methodology.

## Section Summary

- Bank and non-bank SDs face different capital frameworks; minimum $20M for non-bank SDs
- Initial margin covers potential loss; variation margin settles daily mark-to-market changes
- UMR Phases 1-6 (2016-2022) progressively expanded margin requirements to smaller entities
- DeFi collateral systems serve the same function but differ in structure: higher ratios, instant liquidation, no segregation
- Standardized IM schedule ranges from 1% (short-dated rates) to 15% (commodity, equity, other)
