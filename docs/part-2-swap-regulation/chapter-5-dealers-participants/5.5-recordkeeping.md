# 5.5 Recordkeeping & Reporting Obligations

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this section, you will be able to:
    - Enumerate the recordkeeping requirements for registered swap dealers
    - Explain the distinction between real-time public reporting and regulatory reporting
    - Analyze whether blockchain records satisfy CFTC recordkeeping requirements


---

## Swap Dealer Recordkeeping Requirements

CFTC Regulation §23.201-23.205 imposes comprehensive recordkeeping obligations on registered swap dealers:

### What Must Be Recorded

| Record Category | CFTC Rule | Retention Period |
|----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Full transaction records** for every swap | §23.202(a) | 5 years from termination |
| **Daily trading records** including all communications related to swap activity | §23.202(a)(1) | 5 years |
| **Pre-trade communications** (emails, chats, phone calls that precede a swap transaction) | §23.202(b) | 5 years |
| **Voice recordings** for all telephone lines used for swap trading | §23.202(b)(3) | 1 year |
| **Complaints** from counterparties | §23.201(b)(4) | 5 years |
| **Compliance records** including policies, procedures, training | §23.201(b) | 5 years |
| **Financial records** sufficient to demonstrate capital compliance | §23.201(a) | 5 years |

!!! warning "The Voice Recording Requirement"
    Swap dealers must record all telephone conversations on lines used for swap trading. This requirement, modeled on broker-dealer rules, was designed to provide an audit trail for trade disputes and manipulation investigations. For DeFi, there is obviously no voice trading — but the underlying principle (complete communication audit trail) raises questions about Discord, Telegram, and governance forum records.


### Record Format & Accessibility

Records must be:
- **Readily accessible** for the first 2 years
- **Available for inspection** by the CFTC or DOJ upon request
- Maintained in a manner that is **searchable by transaction** and **identifiable by counterparty**
- Stored in a non-alterable format (WORM compliance for electronic records)

## Blockchain as Recordkeeping Infrastructure

The question of whether blockchain records satisfy CFTC recordkeeping requirements is one of the most interesting regulatory technology questions in derivatives law:

| CFTC Requirement | Blockchain Capability | Gap? |
|-----------------|----------------------|------|
| Complete transaction records | Every on-chain swap transaction is permanently recorded | **Likely satisfied** |
| 5-year retention | Blockchain is permanent (exceeds 5 years) | **Exceeded** |
| Non-alterable format | Blockchain immutability (far exceeds WORM) | **Exceeded** |
| Searchable by transaction | Block explorers, indexers, analytics platforms | **Likely satisfied** |
| Identifiable by counterparty | Wallet addresses, but not real-world identity | **Gap** — pseudonymous addresses don't satisfy counterparty identification |
| Pre-trade communications | Not on-chain (Discord, Telegram not captured) | **Significant gap** |
| Voice recordings | N/A for DeFi | Not applicable |

!!! success "Key Insight"
    Blockchain technology actually **exceeds** several CFTC recordkeeping requirements — immutability, permanence, completeness of on-chain transaction records. But it **fails** on counterparty identification (pseudonymous wallets) and pre-trade communications (off-chain governance and coordination). A compliant DeFi swap dealer would need to supplement blockchain records with traditional KYC and communication archiving systems.


---

## Section Summary

- Swap dealers must maintain 5-year records of all transactions, communications, and compliance documentation
- Voice recording requirements apply to all telephone lines used for swap trading
- Blockchain records exceed several CFTC requirements (immutability, permanence, completeness) but fail on counterparty identification and off-chain communication capture
- A compliant DeFi entity would need hybrid recordkeeping: blockchain + traditional KYC + communication archiving
