# Chapter 16: Review Questions

---

**1.** Identify the four pillars of a derivatives compliance program. For each pillar, explain the relevant CFTC rule and describe how a DeFi protocol could implement it.

**2.** CFTC Rule 23.600 requires swap dealers to maintain a "comprehensive risk management program." What are the minimum elements this program must include? How does the rule's annual review requirement translate to a DAO governance context?

**3.** A DeFi protocol developer argues that "code is law" — if the smart contract enforces position limits and collateral requirements, no additional compliance program is needed. Evaluate this argument against CFTC Rule 23.602's "diligent supervision" standard. What compliance functions can smart contracts perform? What functions require human oversight?

**4.** Explain the CCO's personal liability under CFTC Rule 3.3. If a DAO designates a pseudonymous contributor as its "compliance lead," does this satisfy the CCO requirement? What specific regulatory elements are missing?

**5.** A swap dealer's CCO discovers that a senior trader has been engaging in wash trading to inflate volume metrics. Describe the escalation and remediation process under CFTC rules. What happens if the CCO fails to act?

**6.** Compare pre-trade controls and post-trade surveillance. Give two examples of each in TradFi and their DeFi equivalents. Why are both necessary — why can't a compliance program rely solely on pre-trade controls?

**7.** A DeFi derivatives protocol wants to implement trade surveillance using on-chain data. Describe the four-layer surveillance approach (transaction monitoring, wallet clustering, pattern detection, alert generation). What tools would you recommend for each layer, and what is the primary challenge at each layer?

**8.** Explain the "compliance oracle" concept. How does it address both the regulatory requirement for KYC/AML and the DeFi community's concern about user privacy? What are its current limitations?

**9.** Apply the five-layer DeFi Compliance Stack to a hypothetical perpetual futures protocol on Ethereum. For each layer, describe: (a) what would be implemented, (b) who would operate it, and (c) what regulatory requirement it satisfies. Which layers are the highest priority for a protocol launching today?

**10.** The CFTC's enforcement action against Ooki DAO established that a DAO can be a liable entity. If a DAO is liable, it logically needs a compliance program. Design a realistic compliance architecture for a DAO-governed DeFi derivatives protocol that satisfies CFTC requirements while preserving meaningful decentralization. Where are the unavoidable compromises?
