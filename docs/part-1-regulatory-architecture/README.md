# Part I: The Regulatory Architecture

!!! info "What You'll Learn in Part I"
    Part I builds your regulatory foundation — the legal landscape that governs every derivative instrument, every swap contract, and every DeFi protocol that creates derivative-like exposures. Without this foundation, everything else in this book is building on sand.

    You'll learn why derivatives lawyers exist (hint: $600 trillion in notional value and a financial crisis), how the CFTC and SEC divide the regulatory world between them, what the core derivative instruments actually are, and how the Commodity Exchange Act and Dodd-Frank Title VII created the modern regulatory framework that now reaches into DeFi.


By the end of Part I, you will be able to:
- Navigate the CFTC/SEC jurisdictional divide and correctly classify any derivative instrument
- Explain the mechanics and regulatory treatment of futures, options, forwards, and swaps
- Map the post-Dodd-Frank regulatory ecosystem: DCMs, SEFs, SDRs, DCOs, and the NFA
- Trace the legal reasoning that classifies Bitcoin as a commodity and extends CFTC jurisdiction into digital assets
- Understand the CEA and Dodd-Frank Title VII well enough to manage AI that performs detailed regulatory analysis

Part I is your regulatory operating system. Parts II–VI are applications that run on top of it.
