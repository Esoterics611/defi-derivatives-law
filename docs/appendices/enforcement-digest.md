# Appendix E: CFTC DeFi Enforcement Digest

A chronological digest of CFTC enforcement actions relevant to DeFi and digital asset derivatives. This appendix covers actions from 2015 through 2025 that establish precedent for how the CFTC applies derivatives regulation to digital asset markets.

---

## Digital Asset Enforcement Timeline

### 2015–2017: Establishing Jurisdiction

| Date | Respondent | Violation | Penalty | Key Holding | Status | Case Reference |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| Sept 2015 | Coinflip, Inc. d/b/a Derivabit | Operating unregistered SEF; offering off-exchange commodity options | Cease and desist | Bitcoin options are commodity options subject to CEA; platforms facilitating Bitcoin options trading must register | Settled | CFTC Docket No. 15-29 |
| June 2016 | Bitfinex (BFXNA Inc.) | Offering illegal off-exchange financed retail commodity transactions in Bitcoin | $75,000 | Offering leveraged/margined/financed Bitcoin trading to non-ECPs violates CEA § 2(e) — must be on registered DCM | Settled | CFTC Docket No. 16-19 |

### 2018–2020: Expanding Digital Asset Enforcement

| Date | Respondent | Violation | Penalty | Key Holding | Status | Case Reference |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| Sept 2018 | 1pool Ltd. (1Broker) | Offering off-exchange leveraged retail commodity transactions in Bitcoin; operating as unregistered FCM | $1 million (parallel DOJ criminal charges) | Bitcoin-based CFDs are swaps; offering to US retail customers without DCM registration violates CEA | Settled (CFTC); DOJ charges filed | CFTC Docket No. 18-23 |
| Oct 2020 | BitMEX (HDR Global Trading Ltd.) | Operating unregistered trading platform; violating KYC/AML requirements | $100 million (combined with FinCEN) | Operating a derivatives platform accessible to US persons requires CFTC registration regardless of where the platform is incorporated | Settled (2021) | CFTC Case No. 20-33 |

### 2021–2022: The DeFi Enforcement Wave

| Date | Respondent | Violation | Penalty | Key Holding | Status | Case Reference |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| Sept 2021 | Tether / Bitfinex | Multiple violations including false statements about USDT reserves; unlawful off-exchange commodity transactions | $42.5 million (Tether) + $1.5 million (Bitfinex) | Stablecoins used in derivatives transactions are within CFTC jurisdiction; false statements about reserves violate CEA | Settled | CFTC Docket No. 22-04 (Tether), 22-05 (Bitfinex) |
| Sept 2022 | bZeroX, LLC and founders Tom Bean & Kyle Kistner | Operating unregistered trading platform; failing to implement KYC | Consent order | Founders of DeFi protocol personally liable; converting to DAO does not eliminate prior liability | Settled | CFTC Docket No. 22-31 |
| Sept 2022 | Ooki DAO (f/k/a bZx DAO) | Operating unregistered trading platform; failing to implement KYC (as DAO successor to bZeroX) | $643,542 | **Landmark**: DAO can be sued as unincorporated association; token holders who voted on governance proposals may face personal liability | Default judgment | CFTC v. Ooki DAO, No. 3:22-cv-05416 (N.D. Cal.) |

## Enforcement Trend Analysis

### Penalty Escalation

Penalties in digital asset enforcement actions have escalated dramatically:

- **2015-2017**: $0-$75,000 range — warning shots
- **2018-2020**: $1M-$100M range — serious financial consequences
- **2021-2023**: $250K-$42.5M per action — DeFi-specific actions at lower amounts but establishing precedent
- **2024+**: Expected further escalation as precedent is established and protocols grow

### Expanding Jurisdictional Claims

1. **2015**: Bitcoin is a commodity (Coinflip)
2. **2016**: Leveraged Bitcoin trading = retail commodity transaction (Bitfinex)
3. **2018**: Bitcoin CFDs are swaps (1Broker)
4. **2020**: Offshore incorporation doesn't avoid CFTC jurisdiction (BitMEX)
5. **2022**: DAOs can be sued; token holders face personal liability (Ooki DAO)
6. **2022**: Binary options/event contracts are swaps (Polymarket)
7. **2023**: Novel DeFi derivative tokens are swaps, and their platforms must register (Opyn)
8. **2023**: Systematic "DeFi sweep" signals sector-wide enforcement intent

### Key Trends

- **Entity type is irrelevant**: LLC, DAO, offshore corporation — all face enforcement
- **Size doesn't matter**: Both large platforms (BitMEX) and small protocols (Deridex) are targeted
- **Settlements are the norm**: Most cases settle; litigated defenses remain untested
- **Criminal exposure is real**: DOJ has shown willingness to bring criminal charges alongside CFTC civil actions
- **US access is the trigger**: Protocols that restrict US access face lower (but not zero) enforcement risk
