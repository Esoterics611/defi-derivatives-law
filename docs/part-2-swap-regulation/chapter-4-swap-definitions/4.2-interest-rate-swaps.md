# 4.2 Interest Rate Swaps: Vanilla to Exotic

!!! info "Learning Objectives"
    After completing this section, you will be able to:
    - Explain the mechanics of a plain vanilla interest rate swap including payment calculations
    - Distinguish between fixed-for-floating, basis, and overnight index swaps
    - Identify DeFi protocol equivalents that create interest-rate-swap-like exposures
    - Use AI to model swap cash flows and understand where AI output requires validation


!!! warning "Key Terms"
    - **SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate)**: The benchmark replacement for LIBOR, based on overnight Treasury repo transactions; now the dominant USD floating rate reference
    - **Notional Amount**: The hypothetical principal on which swap payments are calculated — never exchanged between counterparties
    - **Fixed Leg**: The stream of payments calculated at a predetermined fixed rate
    - **Floating Leg**: The stream of payments calculated at a variable rate linked to a reference benchmark
    - **Day Count Convention**: The method for calculating the fraction of a year for interest accrual (e.g., Actual/360, 30/360)


---

## The Plain Vanilla Interest Rate Swap

Interest rate swaps (IRS) are the **dominant derivative instrument in global markets**, representing approximately $400 trillion in notional value — roughly 80% of all OTC derivatives outstanding. The plain vanilla IRS is the foundational instrument that every derivatives professional must understand at a mechanical level.

### The Structure

A plain vanilla IRS involves two counterparties exchanging periodic payments on a specified notional amount for a defined term:

- **Counterparty A (the "fixed payer")** pays a fixed rate (e.g., 4.25%) on the notional
- **Counterparty B (the "floating payer")** pays a floating rate (e.g., SOFR + 50 bps) on the same notional

Only the **net difference** is exchanged on each payment date. If the fixed rate obligation exceeds the floating rate obligation, the fixed payer pays the difference to the floating payer — and vice versa.

!!! success "Worked Example"
    **Terms:** $100 million notional, 5-year term, quarterly payments
    - Fixed payer: 4.25% per annum
    - Floating payer: 3-month compounded SOFR

    **Quarter 1 (assume SOFR averages 4.50%):**
    - Fixed payment: $100M × 4.25% × (90/360) = **$1,062,500**
    - Floating payment: $100M × 4.50% × (90/360) = **$1,125,000**
    - Net: Fixed payer **receives** $62,500 (floating rate > fixed rate)

    **Quarter 2 (assume SOFR drops to 3.80%):**
    - Fixed payment: $100M × 4.25% × (90/360) = **$1,062,500**
    - Floating payment: $100M × 3.80% × (90/360) = **$950,000**
    - Net: Fixed payer **pays** $112,500 (fixed rate > floating rate)


### Why Entities Enter IRS

The economic purpose of an IRS is to transform interest rate exposure:

| User | Current Exposure | Desired Exposure | Swap Action |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Corporate borrower with floating-rate loan | Pays SOFR + spread | Wants fixed costs | Pays fixed, receives floating |
| Bank with fixed-rate mortgage portfolio | Receives fixed | Wants floating to match deposits | Pays fixed, receives floating |
| Insurance company | Receives fixed on bond portfolio | Wants to increase yield pickup | Receives floating, pays fixed |
| Speculator | No existing exposure | Believes rates will rise | Pays fixed, receives floating |

### SOFR: The Post-LIBOR World

After 2023, LIBOR is dead. The London Interbank Offered Rate, which underpinned hundreds of trillions in derivatives for decades, was retired due to the LIBOR manipulation scandal and the fundamental fragility of a rate based on estimates rather than transactions. Its replacement, **SOFR**, is published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and is based on actual overnight Treasury repurchase agreement transactions — making it both more robust and fundamentally different in structure.

Key differences for swap practitioners:

| Feature | LIBOR | SOFR |
|---------|-------|------|
| Basis | Bank estimates | Actual transactions |
| Term structure | Built-in (1M, 3M, 6M) | Overnight; term SOFR derived |
| Credit risk component | Yes (bank credit) | No (Treasury collateral) |
| Lookback/lockout needed | No | Yes (rate not known in advance) |

## IRS Variants

**Basis swaps** exchange two floating rates — for example, 1-month SOFR for 3-month SOFR, or SOFR for EURIBOR. These are used to hedge basis risk between different funding rates.

**Overnight Index Swaps (OIS)** exchange a fixed rate for a compounded overnight rate. Post-LIBOR transition, most "plain vanilla" IRS are effectively OIS referencing compounded SOFR.

**Amortizing and accreting swaps** have notional amounts that decrease (amortizing) or increase (accreting) over the life of the swap, matching the cash flow profiles of underlying assets like mortgages or construction loans.

## The DeFi Parallel

Several DeFi protocols create exposure that is economically equivalent to interest rate swaps:

| DeFi Protocol | Mechanism | IRS Equivalent |
|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Pendle Finance** | Splits yield-bearing tokens into principal tokens (PT) and yield tokens (YT) | Buying a YT = receiving floating, paying fixed (the PT discount) |
| **Notional Finance** | Fixed-rate lending/borrowing via fCash tokens | Lending at fixed rate while funding at variable = paying floating, receiving fixed |
| **Element Finance** | Yield token stripping | PT/YT decomposition mirrors fixed/floating leg separation |
| **Aave/Compound variable rates** | Floating supply/borrow rates | The variable rate exposure itself is the floating leg — hedging it requires a swap |

!!! danger "The Regulatory Question"
    If Pendle's yield token (YT) gives the holder the right to receive a floating yield stream in exchange for an upfront fixed payment, is the YT economically a swap? Under CEA §1a(47)(A)(iii) — "provides on an executory basis for the exchange of one or more payments based on the value or level of one or more rates" — the argument is strong. The CFTC has not yet taken action against interest rate DeFi protocols, but the functional equivalence is difficult to deny.


!!! info "AI Practitioner Note"
    IRS cash flow modeling is one of AI's strongest capabilities. Use this prompt:

    *"Calculate the quarterly net cash flows for a 3-year interest rate swap with $50 million notional. Fixed rate: 4.10%, Actual/360 day count. Floating rate: compounded SOFR. Use the following SOFR rate path: [Q1: 4.35%, Q2: 4.20%, Q3: 3.95%, Q4: 3.75%, Q5: 3.60%...]. Show each quarter's fixed payment, floating payment, and net exchange. Then calculate the mark-to-market value at the end of Year 1 assuming a flat SOFR curve at 3.80%."*

    **What to verify:** AI handles the arithmetic well but frequently uses incorrect day count conventions (applying 30/360 when you specified Actual/360) or forgets compounding for SOFR calculations. Always check the day count fraction in the first payment calculation — if it's wrong there, every number downstream is wrong.

## Section Summary

- Interest rate swaps represent ~$400 trillion in notional — 80% of all OTC derivatives
- The plain vanilla IRS exchanges fixed payments for floating payments on a notional amount
- SOFR has replaced LIBOR as the dominant USD floating rate benchmark
- IRS variants include basis swaps, OIS, amortizing/accreting structures
- DeFi protocols (Pendle, Notional, Element) create economically equivalent exposures
- The regulatory question of whether DeFi yield-splitting constitutes a "swap" remains open but analytically strong
