# Chapter 14: Review Questions

---

**1.** Explain the difference between payment netting and close-out netting. Why does payment netting reduce operational risk while close-out netting reduces credit risk?

**2.** A derivatives dealer has 500 outstanding swap transactions with a single counterparty under one ISDA Master Agreement. The gross notional exposure is $8 billion, but after close-out netting, the net exposure is $400 million. Explain why this 95% reduction matters for capital requirements under Basel III.

**3.** Identify three Events of Default under ISDA Section 5(a) and explain the grace period (if any) associated with each. Why does the Bankruptcy Event of Default have no grace period?

**4.** Compare Automatic Early Termination (AET) and optional early termination. In which jurisdictions is AET commonly elected, and why?

**5.** Explain the difference between the "Market Quotation" method (1992 ISDA) and the "Close-out Amount" method (2002 ISDA). Why did the industry move to the newer standard after the 2008 financial crisis?

**6.** DeFi achieves "netting-like" outcomes through atomic settlement and composability. Explain why these technological solutions do not provide the same legal protections as ISDA close-out netting. What specific legal infrastructure is missing?

**7.** A DeFi user has a long ETH perpetual on Protocol A and a short ETH perpetual on Protocol B. Both protocols experience a governance attack and halt trading. In TradFi, these positions might be netted under a single Master Agreement. Why can't they be netted in DeFi? What would need to change for cross-protocol netting to be legally enforceable?

**8.** Explain the purpose of 11 U.S.C. §560 (the swap agreement safe harbor). If a DeFi protocol operator files for bankruptcy, could a liquidity provider invoke §560 to close out their position? Identify at least two legal obstacles.

**9.** In the Terra/Luna collapse, losses were allocated by "transaction speed rather than legal priority." Explain what this means in practice. How would the outcome have differed if Terra's positions had been governed by ISDA Master Agreements with bankruptcy safe harbor protections?

**10.** A bankruptcy trustee argues that a DeFi protocol's automated liquidation of a user's collateral — which occurred 30 minutes after the user filed for Chapter 11 — violated the automatic stay under §362. The protocol operator argues the liquidation was a valid exercise of safe harbor rights under §560. Evaluate both arguments.
