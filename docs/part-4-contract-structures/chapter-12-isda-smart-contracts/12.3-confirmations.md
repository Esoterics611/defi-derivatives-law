# 12.3 Confirmations & Transaction-Level Documentation

!!! info "Section Objectives"
    After completing this section, you will be able to:

    - Explain the role of Confirmations in the ISDA documentation framework
    - Identify the ISDA Definitions booklets and their function
    - Distinguish between long-form and short-form confirmations
    - Map Confirmation elements to DeFi protocol trade parameters

---

## The Confirmation's Role

A Confirmation is the transaction-specific document that records the economic terms of an individual derivative trade. While the Master Agreement and Schedule establish the legal framework for the relationship, the Confirmation specifies **what was actually traded**: notional amount, tenor, fixed rate, floating rate index, payment frequency, calculation methodology, and any trade-specific elections.

Every new trade generates a new Confirmation. A single Master Agreement relationship between two major banks may have thousands of outstanding Confirmations at any time, each representing a different swap, option, or structured transaction.

## ISDA Definitions Booklets

Rather than defining every term from scratch in each Confirmation, ISDA publishes standardized **Definitions booklets** that Confirmations incorporate by reference. These booklets contain hundreds of pages of precisely defined terms, calculation conventions, and fallback provisions for specific product types:

| Definitions Booklet | Product Coverage | Key Provisions |
|---------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| **2021 ISDA Interest Rate Derivatives Definitions** | IRS, basis swaps, caps, floors, swaptions | SOFR fallbacks (replacing LIBOR), compounding conventions, day count fractions, payment date adjustments |
| **2014 ISDA Credit Derivatives Definitions** | CDS, index CDS, credit-linked notes | Credit event definitions (bankruptcy, failure to pay, restructuring), auction settlement mechanics, successor provisions |
| **1998 FX and Currency Option Definitions** | FX options, NDFs, barrier options | Settlement terms, disruption events, currency fallbacks |
| **2002 ISDA Equity Derivatives Definitions** | Equity swaps, equity options, variance swaps | Extraordinary events (mergers, delistings), disrupted day provisions, correction periods |
| **2005 ISDA Commodity Definitions** | Commodity swaps, commodity options | Pricing source specifications, market disruption events, weather derivatives provisions |

The 2021 Interest Rate Definitions booklet is particularly significant: it replaced the 2006 Definitions and includes built-in SOFR and risk-free rate fallbacks following the global LIBOR transition. Any DeFi protocol referencing interest rates should understand these conventions, as they define how the TradFi world calculates interest payments.

## Long-Form vs. Short-Form Confirmations

**Long-form Confirmations** include all economic terms and relevant definitions within the Confirmation itself. They are used for bespoke or unusual transactions where standard definitions may not adequately capture the trade's mechanics.

**Short-form Confirmations** reference the applicable ISDA Definitions booklet and specify only the variable terms (notional, rate, dates). The definitions booklet fills in everything else by reference. Short-form Confirmations are typically 2-5 pages; long-form can exceed 30 pages.

**Master Confirmation Agreements (MCAs)** go one step further: for counterparties that repeatedly trade the same product type, an MCA establishes the standard terms once, and individual trades are documented by brief "Transaction Supplements" that specify only the variable economic terms. This is analogous to a DeFi protocol where users open positions by specifying amount and direction — the protocol's code handles everything else.

## Confirmation Elements vs. DeFi Trade Parameters

| Confirmation Element | IRS Example | CDS Example | DeFi Protocol Parameter |
|---------------------|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Notional Amount** | $100,000,000 | $50,000,000 (Reference Obligation) | Position size (e.g., 10 ETH, $50,000 USDC margin) |
| **Trade Date** | March 15, 2025 | March 15, 2025 | Block timestamp of transaction |
| **Effective Date** | March 17, 2025 (T+2) | March 20, 2025 (IMM date) | Immediate (same block) |
| **Termination Date** | March 17, 2030 (5-year) | December 20, 2029 (IMM date) | No termination (perpetual) or user-defined close |
| **Fixed Rate** | 4.25% per annum | 100 bps per annum (premium) | Funding rate (recalculated every 8 hours) |
| **Floating Rate** | 3M SOFR compounded in arrears | N/A (event-triggered) | Oracle price feed |
| **Payment Frequency** | Quarterly | Quarterly | Continuous (funding rate) or per-block |
| **Day Count Fraction** | Actual/360 | Actual/360 | Block-based (no day count convention) |
| **Business Day Convention** | Modified Following | Modified Following | Not applicable — blockchain operates 24/7/365 |
| **Calculation Agent** | Goldman Sachs | JPMorgan | Oracle + smart contract logic |

!!! info "AI Practitioner Note"
    **Parsing an ISDA confirmation to extract key economic terms:**

    *"Parse the following ISDA Confirmation and extract: (1) the product type, (2) notional amount, (3) trade date and effective date, (4) termination/maturity date, (5) fixed and floating rates, (6) payment frequency, (7) day count fractions, (8) calculation agent, (9) any embedded options or triggers, and (10) the applicable ISDA Definitions booklet. Present the extracted terms in a standardized table. Flag any non-standard or unusual provisions that deviate from market convention for this product type."*

    **What to verify:** AI models are generally accurate at extracting plain-text economic terms but struggle with embedded optionality (e.g., a callable swap where the Confirmation includes a Bermuda swaption), lookback periods for floating rate calculations, and complex compounding conventions. If the AI does not identify embedded options in a structured Confirmation, re-prompt specifically for optionality analysis.

---

## Section Summary

- Confirmations document the economic terms of individual trades within the Master Agreement framework
- ISDA Definitions booklets provide standardized terms that Confirmations incorporate by reference
- MCAs and Transaction Supplements streamline documentation for repeat product types
- DeFi trade parameters map to Confirmation elements but with key structural differences: instant settlement, no maturity dates (perpetuals), continuous funding rates, and oracle-based pricing
