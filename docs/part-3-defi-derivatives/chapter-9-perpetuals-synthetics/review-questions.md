# Review Questions — Chapter 9: Perpetual Futures, Synthetics & Prediction Markets

---

**1.** Explain the funding rate mechanism in perpetual futures. How does it replace the delivery/expiry function of traditional futures? Could the funding rate itself be classified as a separate swap instrument (a basis swap between perp and spot prices)?

**2.** Rank the following DeFi derivatives instruments by CFTC enforcement risk from highest to lowest: (a) perpetual futures on ETH, (b) synthetic gold tokens, (c) binary options on election outcomes, (d) covered call options vaults, (e) synthetic Apple stock tokens. Explain your ranking.

**3.** dYdX processes $1–3 billion in daily perpetual futures volume. The CME processes $4–6 billion in daily crypto derivatives volume. Analyze the systemic risk implications of having an unregistered derivatives market that rivals the regulated market in size. What happens if a major DeFi perp protocol experiences a liquidity crisis?

**4.** A DeFi protocol launches a synthetic asset that tracks the S&P 500 index. Users deposit USDC as collateral and receive synthetic S&P 500 tokens. The protocol uses Chainlink oracles for pricing. Classify this instrument under the CEA. Is it a CFTC-jurisdiction swap (broad-based index) or an SEC-jurisdiction security-based swap? What additional facts would you need to determine jurisdiction?

**5.** The Kalshi ruling allowed election contracts on registered DCMs. Does this ruling benefit or harm DeFi prediction markets like Polymarket? Argue both sides.

**6.** Opyn's Squeeth tracks ETH² — the square of ETH's price. This creates a convexity payoff that is economically similar to a perpetual option. Analyze whether Squeeth is: (a) a swap under §1a(47), (b) a commodity option under §4c(b), or (c) a novel instrument that doesn't fit neatly into existing categories. Does the classification matter for regulatory purposes?

**7.** Polymarket settled with the CFTC in 2022, geo-blocked U.S. users, and then became the most visible prediction market in the world during the 2024 election. Evaluate the CFTC's enforcement strategy: was the $1.4M settlement effective? What should the CFTC's next move be?

**8.** DeFi Options Vaults (DOVs) automate covered call writing. When a user deposits ETH into a DOV that writes weekly covered calls: (a) who is the "writer" of the option — the vault depositor, the vault smart contract, or the vault operator? (b) Under CEA §4c(b), who is offering unregistered commodity options? Analyze the liability chain.

**9.** Using the dYdX case study, design a regulatory migration strategy for a hypothetical DeFi perp protocol currently operating as a U.S. entity. What corporate, technical, and operational changes would you recommend? What residual regulatory risks would remain even after full migration?

**10.** Flash-forward to 2030: the CFTC has issued clearing determinations for BTC and ETH perpetual futures. All perps must be cleared through registered DCOs and traded on registered DCMs. What happens to the DeFi perp ecosystem? Model two scenarios: (a) DeFi protocols comply by integrating with registered infrastructure, and (b) DeFi protocols refuse to comply and continue operating offshore.
